# How to Play

Open `Assets/Scenes/RhythmPrototype.unity` in Unity and press **Play**.

- Press **1** to start the Tutorial and practice taps, holds, drag notes, and two math questions.
- Basic and Orbit each last two minutes and contain 10 math questions, spaced about 12 seconds apart.
- When an equation appears, wait for the correct numbered note to reach the judgment line, then hit it. Play the regular rhythm notes between questions.
- Use **D / F / J / K** or click the matching lane. Hold long notes until their tails cross the line. For diamond notes, hold the matching lane before the note reaches the line.
- Press **A** for autoplay, **R** to retry, **Esc** to return to level selection, **M** to mute, and **V** to reduce motion.

## Audio Synchronization

Press **C** to open calibration. Listen to the count-in, then tap **Space** eight times with the clicks to estimate the timing offset.

Use **Left / Right** to align the flashing block with the sound in 10 ms steps. A positive offset delays the visuals.

Select **SAVE / BACK** or press **Esc** to save. The offset affects both note visuals and judgment timing.
